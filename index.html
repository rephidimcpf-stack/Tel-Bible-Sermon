01 Top bar and settings

Always visible. On a phone the settings strip hides behind ☰.

ButtonWhat it does
Bible Switches to the reading screen. It remembers the book and chapter you were last on, and reading aloud stops if it was running.
Sermons Switches to the sermon library and editor.
★ Saved Lists every verse you bookmarked, followed by every commentary note you have written. Tapping any row jumps to that verse.
☰ Opens and closes the settings strip. Only shown on screens narrower than 900px; on a wide screen the strip is always open.
Theme drop-down Auto, Paper, Sepia, Forest, Dark, Midnight or High contrast. Auto follows the phone's own light/dark setting and keeps following it live.
Telugu font drop-down Seven Telugu typefaces: Noto Sans Telugu, Noto Serif Telugu, Hind Guntur, Mandali, Ramabhadra, Gidugu, Suranna. Changes the whole app immediately.
A− A+ Text size for the whole app, 14px up to 40px in steps of 2. Separate from the presentation size.
Word notes checkbox When ticked, tapping any single word in a verse lets you attach a note to that word. When unticked, words are plain text and only words that already carry a note stay tappable.
↓ Backup Downloads one .json file holding everything: sermons, trash, commentary, word notes, verse edits, highlights, bookmarks, series colours and settings. Keep it somewhere safe.
↑ Restore Opens the file picker for a backup or a single sermon .json, then asks how to apply it — see Merge / Replace below.
Merge Replace Cancel Merge keeps what is already on the device and adds only what is new or newer — the safe choice. Replace wipes this device and uses the backup exactly.

The strip above the text, and the controls inside a chapter.

Book Chapter drop-downs All 66 books, then the chapters of that book. Changing the book lands you on its first chapter.

Search Opens the full-text Bible search. The / key does the same.
Refs Shows or hides the cross-reference line under each verse. Lit up when on, which is the default. Your choice is remembered.
Highlight Opens the colour strip. The label changes to Highlight on while a colour is armed, and any verse you tap from then on is painted in it.
■ ×8 colour swatches Yellow, green, blue, pink, orange, purple, teal, grey. Tapping the same colour on an already-coloured verse removes it.
Off Disarms highlighting so tapping a verse goes back to normal behaviour.
16 verse number Opens the verse dialog for that verse — note, highlight, copy, listen, add to sermon.
note tag Marks a verse that has commentary. Opens it in a small popover where you can read, edit or delete it.
★ Shows that a verse is bookmarked. Bookmarks appear under the ★ Saved tab.
underlined word A word carrying its own note is dotted-underlined. Tap it to read, edit or delete that note.
▸ Title sermon pin A sermon whose reference matches this verse appears above it. Tapping presents that sermon. If several match, one line opens to show them all.
Reading position is saved on its own — close the app mid-chapter and it reopens exactly there.

03 Verse dialog

Opened by tapping a verse number. Everything about one verse in a single sheet.

ButtonWhat it does
Related verses rows Up to eight cross-references, strongest first, each with the opening words of that verse. Tapping one shows it in a popup without moving you.
■ ×8 ✕ Sets this verse's highlight colour; ✕ removes it.
B I U S Bold, italic, underline, strike-through for the commentary you are writing.
A ×3 Highlight the selected commentary text in yellow, green or blue.
• 1. Bulleted and numbered lists inside the commentary. Press Enter on an empty bullet to step back out of the list.
Aa Clears all formatting from the selection.
▶ Listen Starts reading aloud from this verse and carries on to the end of the chapter.
Copy Copies the verse text followed by its reference, ready to paste anywhere.
+ Add to sermon Drops this verse into the sermon you are writing, as a quotation with its reference. If no sermon is open it starts a new one holding just this verse.
Save Stores the commentary and any change to the verse text, then refreshes the chapter. Close discards instead.
Editing the verse text is for this device only. It never alters the underlying Bible data, and clearing it restores the original wording.

04 Related verses and cross-reference chips

The ⇄ line under a verse, and the popup behind it.

ButtonWhat it does
⇄ రోమా 5:8 Opens a popup with the full Telugu text of that verse. You stay on the chapter you were reading. A range such as 4:9–10 shows every verse in it, numbered.
+5 more Opens the verse dialog, where the complete list of eight is shown with snippets.
Open in Bible Inside the popup — actually travels to that verse in its own chapter. The one button that moves you.
Copy Close Copies the verse with its reference, or dismisses the popup. Tapping outside it also closes.
References come from the OpenBible.info dataset (CC BY), filtered to links that scored 5 or more votes and checked against this Telugu text, so nothing points at a verse that does not exist here.

05 Bible search

ButtonWhat it does
Search Telugu text… Searches as you type once you have two characters. Put words in "quotes" to find that exact phrase.
✕ Clears the box and the results and puts the cursor back.
All పాత · OT క్రొత్త · NT Narrows the search to the whole Bible, the Old Testament or the New Testament. Re-runs the search immediately.

08 Presentation mode

On a computer the controls sit in a strip along the top. On a phone they become a dock at the bottom, within reach of your thumb, holding only back, forward and the page number — everything else waits behind ⋯. The dock fades after about three seconds so nothing competes with the text.

ButtonWhat it does
3 / 12 2:41 Which slide you are on, and how long the talk has been running. Neither can be tapped.
⋯ Phone only. Opens the rest of the controls — timer, Bible/Sermon, text size, Blank, Full and ✕ — in a row just above the dock. It closes by itself once you use one, or when the dock fades.
‹ › Back and forward one slide — or one chapter, when presenting the Bible. On a phone these are the two large buttons at the bottom corners.
Bible / Sermon Swaps what is on screen between the sermon and the Bible chapter, without leaving presentation.
A− A+ Presentation text size, 16px to 72px in steps of 3. Kept for next time.
Blank Black screen, for prayer or a story when you do not want eyes on the text. The slide and timer carry on underneath. Tap the black screen — or press B — to bring it back.
Full True full screen, hiding the browser's own bars. F toggles it too.
✕ Leaves presentation and returns to the editor. Esc does the same.
On a phone a tap anywhere on the slide moves forward one step and brings the dock back with it; a tap on the left edge goes back, and a swipe left or right does the same. In Bible mode the book and chapter pickers appear only while the controls are showing, so the text gets the whole screen.

09 Listen — the voice reader

The bar that appears along the bottom while a chapter is being read.

ButtonWhat it does
యోహాను 3:16 The verse being spoken right now. Not a button.
⏮ ⏭ Skips back or forward one verse and starts speaking it.
⏸ ▶ Pauses and resumes. When nothing is playing, ▶ starts the chapter from the beginning.
■ Stops completely and clears the highlight.
⚙ Speed 0.7× to 1.3×, scroll-to-follow on or off, carry on into the next chapter on or off, and the list of voices installed on the device.
One thing to know: the reading uses the phone's own speech engine. If no Telugu voice is installed it says so once and falls back to the default voice — the ⚙ panel explains where to add one. Changing chapter, pressing ‹ › or leaving the Bible tab stops the reading.

12 Things that happen without a button

Saving

Everything saves itself. Sermons, notes, highlights, bookmarks and settings are written about a second after you stop typing, and again if you close the app. There is no Save button to forget.

Series codes and colours

Filling in Book and Reference generates a series code — Genesis 1 becomes SER-0001, Revelation 22 becomes SER-1189. Each series keeps one colour, and the number may change without the colour ever changing.

References inside your writing

When you type something like “John 3:16” in a sermon or a note, it turns into a tappable reference by itself, and tapping it opens the verse rather than moving you.

Touching a verse

On a computer, moving the mouse over a verse tints it. On a phone, where there is no hover, a tap flashes the verse briefly instead — the same confirmation, without anything staying stuck.

Reading aloud

The verse being spoken is highlighted and the page follows it down the chapter, so you can look away and come back.
